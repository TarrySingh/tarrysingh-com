# Visual storyboard — 38 distinct figures

Prepared 9 September 2026. All figures are planned, not built. The commission is 28 two-dimensional figures and 10 genuine three-dimensional figures. Evidence sources refer to IDs in [research-ledger.md](research-ledger.md). The machine-readable brief is [production-manifest.json](production-manifest.json).

The visual standard is explanatory precision with a recognisable Synaptic identity. A 3D scene must explain spatial structure or a clearly labelled conceptual relationship. It cannot stand in for a proof or experimental result. 2D diagrams should carry comparisons and arguments that become harder to read in perspective.

The five flagship scientific views are the fluid field, zeta landscape, matched protein structures, crystal lattice and reservoir cross-section. The agent-lab view is a transparent scenario, visually distinct from measured evidence. Company engineering views use documented educational geometry; they do not pretend to expose proprietary product designs.

## Universal acceptance conditions

- Every figure has one claim or explanatory purpose, a dated source or explicit scenario model, a caption, an accessible description and a readable static state.
- Every numerical axis has units and a definition. Data, model outputs and illustrative assumptions are labelled differently in text as well as colour.
- Controls have useful defaults, reset, keyboard access and touch support. The central explanation remains available without interaction.
- Honour reduced motion; provide manual progression and pause. Render inactive scenes only when needed and release GPU resources when unmounted.
- Use responsive fixed proportions and HTML captions to preserve chapter positions. Avoid an empty space while a scientific figure loads.
- Do not use tiny labels, decorative progress indicators, arbitrary scores, random live counters or unsupported precision.
- Molecular and materials assets retain accessions, source versions and reuse terms. Mathematical samples retain equations, precision and generation settings.
- No visual is marked complete until its evidence, rendering, interaction and fallback have been checked. No generated illustration is counted as a data visual.
