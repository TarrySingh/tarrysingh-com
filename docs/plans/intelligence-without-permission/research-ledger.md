## Claims to investigate, not publish as findings yet

- **Worldwide research access:** construct an explicitly dated availability matrix using official country lists, prices, access tiers, rate limits, model cards and scientific-use conditions. Include connectivity, language and purchasing power from appropriate official datasets. Do not invent country rankings or equate a free chatbot with a complete laboratory.
- **Small laboratories outperforming corporate R&D:** find matched tasks, comparable inputs, independent evaluation, full costs and evidence of adoption. The hypothetical challenger can be analysed before it exists, but must remain a scenario.
- **A million agents:** distinguish concurrent active workers, total attempts, software processes, unique models and token counts. Obtain hardware, throughput and coordination assumptions. Investigate correlated errors and duplicated search. Do not extrapolate linear capability from a small-team benchmark.
- **Medicine:** verify any named drug or clinical claim against the original trial record, paper and regulator status. Include unsuccessful programmes and downstream costs. No treatment recommendations are part of this article.
- **Industrial change:** obtain workflow-level evidence in oil and gas, manufacturing and professional services. Separate shorter analysis from shorter certification, construction, rollout or payback.
- **Markets:** distinguish lower input costs, customer price reductions, retained incumbent margins, new entry and new demand. Use company filings and observable contracts where possible; scenario assumptions remain exposed.
- **Institutional inertia:** support claims about grants, promotion, procurement, research access or publishing delays with actual policies and measured examples. Make room for institutions already changing.
- **Historical age:** date the question actually being discussed. The age of an equation, a conjecture, a formalisation project and a prize designation need not be the same.
- **Priority disputes:** seek primary statements and independent assessment if included. Attribute competing accounts. Do not settle allegations from a single company's account or social-media summaries.
