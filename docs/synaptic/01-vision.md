# 01 · Vision

## What we are building

A single, exquisitely-made online presentation of two deep-tech proposals — **MEMPHIS** and **SYMPHONY** — that doubles as Tarry Singh's deep-tech credibility statement to two audiences simultaneously:

1. **EU programme reviewers** (EIC Pathfinder, ERC, Horizon Europe panellists).
2. **Deep-tech VCs and technical operators**.

This is not a portfolio site. It is a science-grade publication that happens to live on the open web.

## Why now

The two proposals are credible, novel, and rare:

- **MEMPHIS** addresses post-von-Neumann computing through hippocampal-inspired memristive hardware — a problem currently overcrowded with marketing but starved of biologically-grounded, decision-milestoned consortia.
- **SYMPHONY** addresses the structural ceiling of LLM-based code understanding by transposing neuromodulation from perceptual to symbolic domains — a defensible, falsifiable thesis at exactly the moment the SWE-bench narrative is publicly cracking.

Both are pitched at TRL 1–4 with concrete success thresholds. Both have lead investigators with peer-reviewed track record (Ramaswamy, Siciliano). Neither is currently easy to find online in a form that matches the quality of the underlying science. The site closes that gap.

## What success looks like

In descending order of importance:

1. **A senior EU programme officer forwards the URL to a panel chair** with no commentary other than the URL.
2. **A deep-tech VC requests a 30-minute meeting** within a week of visiting.
3. **A peer scientist in the field links to it** from social media or a Slack channel.
4. **A journalist cites a phrase from the site** in coverage of neuromorphic / AI-for-code work.

We do not measure success in pageviews, time-on-page, or conversion rates. Those numbers will be incidental. We measure success in the quality of the inbound that follows the launch.

## What we are explicitly not doing

- We are **not** building a SaaS product, a sign-up flow, a freemium tier, or a "join the beta" funnel.
- We are **not** writing marketing copy.
- We are **not** trying to be "approachable to a general audience". A 12-year-old should not be able to follow it, and that is fine.
- We are **not** competing with Vercel-tier developer-marketing sites. We are competing with *Wellcome Collection* online plates, *Nautilus* longform, and the better corners of *Quanta Magazine*.

## The single sentence

If the entire site could only contain one italic line, it would read:

> *A planisphere and an anatomy of two ideas that should not yet exist.*

Every page on the site is a defence of that sentence.

## What the site is, structurally

Five reading paths, each demanding ≤ 4 minutes:

1. **Cover** — title, two plates as hero cards, one paragraph of editorial copy, one navigation choice.
2. **MEMPHIS** — interactive plate, five §1.2.2 advances, decision milestones M12/M18/M24/M30/M33, italic close.
3. **SYMPHONY** — interactive planisphere, twelve task sectors, five objectives O1–O5, italic close.
4. **About the consortium** — partners (Real AI, Newcastle/Ramaswamy, UNINA/Siciliano, UP Robotics) with one-line credentials each.
5. **Contact** — Tarry's email, optional Calendly. No form.

A reviewer should be able to land on path #2 or #3 directly from a deep link in an email and feel oriented within five seconds.
