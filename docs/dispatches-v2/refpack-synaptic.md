## The shared design DNA (this is what to extract)

Every plate is the *same world*. That coherence is the asset — name it and reproduce it:

- **Canvas:** deep navy-to-near-black ink, with a subtle radial light-bloom (a "glowing core" or low horizon "sun") behind the central object. Never flat black — always a graded field with depth.
- **Display type:** oversized **Gloock** (serif display), cream `#f6ead0`-ish, filling the upper third. Often all-caps for the plate name (MEMPHIS, SYMPHONY, UP ROBOTICS), title-case for sub-headers.
- **Micro-labels:** **IBM Plex Mono**, uppercase, wide `~0.22em` tracking, low-contrast (muted cream / slate) — the "PLATE I · MMXXVI · …" kickers, axis labels, figure numbers. This is the signature that makes it read as a *plate*, not a webpage.
- **Accent:** a single warm gold/copper glow `#c9a96e`-ish for the active/illuminated element — the lit node, the localisation sweep, the modulator rail. Used sparingly, as light, never as fill.
- **The central object:** one precise, instrument-grade diagram per plate (a chip lattice, a radar planisphere, a kinematic arm, a cortical column, a Greek pediment, an industrial cell). Technical, exact, slightly cool — like a museum plate or an engineering schematic, not an illustration.
- **Composition:** generous oceanic whitespace (darkspace), centred or radial symmetry, a framed inner panel with a hairline border + faint corner ticks.
- **Mood:** reverent, exact, quietly luminous. "Deep-tech proposal bound as a museum folio."

If a v2 cover plate hits those seven, it belongs to this world. If it looks like stock generative noise, it doesn't.

## Design decision — §10.1 revised (DECIDED 2026-05-28)

The original lock was *generative sumi-e primary, Synaptic abstract secondary*. After this reference-pack review, **Tarry chose option 3 — FUSE.**

> **Cover system = slug-deterministic FUSION:** a sumi-e / ink-wash brush **textural base** carrying a foreground **instrument-object in the Synaptic plate grammar** — graded navy-ink field + soft radial light-bloom, a single warm gold-copper glow used as *light* (never fill), IBM Plex Mono micro-label kickers. The Synaptic plates are the **primary** visual reference; sumi-e provides the brush texture underneath. Brush + schematic.

The two candidates not taken, for the record:
1. Keep sumi-e primary (plates demoted to secondary `Studio`-only texture).
2. Synaptic-plate-derived art *only* (no sumi-e base).

Both §10.1 and the §9 prompt in `Dispatches-v2-Design-Brief.md` are updated to this fusion direction.
