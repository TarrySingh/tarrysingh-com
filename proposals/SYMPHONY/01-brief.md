# SYMPHONY — 90-second brief

**Full name (working):** SYMPHONY — a neuromimetic knowledge substrate for software systems.

**One-sentence pitch.** A nervous system for software: a multi-scale, graph-resident representation of a codebase whose activation patterns are reconfigured on demand by low-bandwidth, task-specific neuromodulatory signals — the first instantiation of biological neuromodulation in a discrete symbolic domain.

**Why now.** LLM-based code understanding has hit a public ceiling: SWE-bench Verified scores collapse from 80%+ headlines to single-digit-to-30% rates under independent re-evaluation (ICSE 2025, ICLR 2026). Static-analysis and architecture-knowledge-graph tools recover what is explicit but cannot recover *why* — the hardest tier of software knowledge, recognised since Avgeriou et al. (2007). SYMPHONY's advance is not to improve either family but to combine their information content under a different organising principle drawn from biology.

**Three advances beyond the state of the art.**

| # | Advance | Departure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| i | Multi-layer extraction into a single substrate | Structural, behavioural, historical, rationale layers unified in one graph-resident representation built for *activation-based* retrieval, not query-based. |
| ii | Context-dependent activation | A single substrate state that surfaces different subnetworks on a task token — using the four-scale neuromodulatory primitives of Mei, Muller & Ramaswamy (2022). |
| iii | Low-bandwidth task control | A narrow scalar control surface — not a prompt window — derived from Siciliano-school haptic shared control. Composable, auditable, bounded. |

**Five objectives O1–O5.**

| ID | Objective | Threshold | Milestone | Lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| O1 | Multi-layer extraction pipeline | ≥ 90% functions, ≥ 80% inter-module deps | M12 | Real AI / UP Robotics |
| O2 | Neuromodulatory reconfiguration | F1 ≥ 0.6 vs. expert ground-truth, p < 0.01 | M18 | Newcastle / Ramaswamy |
| O3 | Low-bandwidth task control | Switch < 500ms, state-preservation ≥ 0.95 | M24 | CREATEPRISMA / Siciliano |
| O4 | Benchmarked advantage | ≥ 20% F1, ≥ 15% actionability vs. baselines | M30 | Real AI + external panel |
| O5 | Equitable-access user study | p < 0.05 for under-represented strata, no stratum disadvantage | M33 | Newcastle ethics + Real AI |

**Critical uncertainty.** Whether multi-scale neuromodulation — demonstrated in continuous perceptual and motor domains — transfers to a symbolic and structural domain (source code) where signals are discrete, hierarchical, linguistic.

**Closing line.** *Same substrate. Different harmonies.*
