# Compliance with ethical principles and relevant legislations  (≤ 5,000 chars)  ·  4,864

SYMPHONY complies in full with the ethical principles set out in Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2021/695 establishing Horizon Europe, with the Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union, and with the national legal frameworks of every jurisdiction in which project tasks are performed (the Netherlands for Real AI, Italy for UNINA-CREATEPRISMA, Croatia for UP Robotics, and the United Kingdom for Newcastle University under post-Brexit arrangements equivalent to EU data-protection law).

**Human subjects (O5 user study).** The user study with at least 60 software engineers, stratified by gender, career stage, and native-language proficiency, will be conducted under the ethical oversight of Newcastle University's Faculty of Science, Agriculture & Engineering Ethics Committee, with approval secured before any recruitment activity. Participation is strictly voluntary; written informed consent will be obtained prior to enrolment, in line with the EU Charter Articles 1 (human dignity), 3 (right to integrity of the person), and 21 (non-discrimination). Participants may withdraw at any time without justification or penalty, with full retraction of associated data on request. The non-inferiority margin on the secondary endpoint (30-day retention) is pre-registered specifically to prevent any stratum being disadvantaged by the intervention; the analysis plan, sample-size calculation, and primary and secondary endpoints will be lodged in a public registry (Open Science Framework or equivalent) before recruitment commences. The protocol prohibits deception, undue inducement, or the recruitment of minors or vulnerable adults.

**Personal data protection.** All processing of personal data is governed by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) for EU activities (the Netherlands, Italy, Croatia) and the corresponding UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 for Newcastle. Lawful basis is informed consent under Article 6(1)(a) and, where relevant, scientific research under Article 89. Data minimisation, purpose limitation, pseudonymisation at collection, and storage limitation are applied throughout. A consortium-level Data Protection Impact Assessment will be completed at M3, with designated Data Protection Officers at Newcastle and at Real AI. No special-category data are processed. International transfers between consortium partners are conducted under EU Standard Contractual Clauses (or the UK equivalent for Newcastle).

**Industrial codebase confidentiality.** The proprietary codebase contributed by UP Robotics (Croatia) for O4 industrial validation is handled under a consortium NDA and an information-security policy aligned with ISO/IEC 27001. Access is restricted to named consortium members; derived outputs are reviewed for sensitive content prior to publication, and UP Robotics retains explicit veto over the disclosure of any code excerpt.

**EU AI Act and Trustworthy AI.** SYMPHONY's outputs constitute fundamental research at TRL 1 to 4 and do not deploy an AI system within the scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. The project nonetheless applies the European Commission's Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI across all seven requirements: human agency and oversight (the low-bandwidth task-control interface is auditable by design); technical robustness (pre-registered evaluation with external advisory panel); privacy and data governance (above); transparency (open-source release); diversity and non-discrimination (O5 stratification); societal and environmental well-being (the equitable-access endpoint); and accountability (a consortium Ethics Manager appointed at M1).

**Non-EU activity (Newcastle, United Kingdom).** All Newcastle-led activities, principally O2 and O5, comply with the UK GDPR, the UK Data Protection Act 2018, and the Concordat to Support Research Integrity, recognised as equivalent to the EU regime under the Commission's adequacy decisions. All such activities are permitted in at least one EU Member State, satisfying Horizon Europe's non-EU-country requirement.

**Research integrity, openness, and conflicts of interest.** The consortium adheres to the European Code of Conduct for Research Integrity (ALLEA, revised 2023). Peer-reviewed publications will be released under CC-BY licences and FAIR data deposited in trusted repositories (Zenodo, OpenAIRE), per Horizon Europe's open-science policy. Any consortium member with a competing financial interest in a baseline system used in O4 will recuse themselves from adjudication; the three-person external advisory panel appointed at M6 provides independent oversight of the benchmark process.

**Ongoing oversight.** The consortium Ethics Manager maintains an active issues register, reports to the General Assembly at every six-month review, and serves as the single point of contact for the Project Officer on any ethics matter raised during the project lifecycle.
